Data sources

ClearFlow is built on public water-system data. Here are the specific sources we use, how they connect, and where the data comes from.

EPA and utility data sources

EPA Community Water System service-area layer

ArcGIS-hosted service-area boundaries for public water systems across the U.S. This is the geographic layer used to match an address (via geocoding and point-in-polygon) to the PWSID most likely serving it.

https://www.epa.gov/community-water-systems

EPA SDWIS / ECHO

Safe Drinking Water Information System and Enforcement and Compliance History Online. Provides public compliance, monitoring, and reporting data for each PWSID.

https://echo.epa.gov/

EPA Consumer Confidence Reports (CCR)

Annual water quality reports published by community water systems. ClearFlow links directly to your system's CCR when available.

https://sdwis.epa.gov/fylccr/

Methodology

1

Step 1 — Geocode

Your address is converted to a latitude/longitude using a geocoding service.

2

Step 2 — Point-in-polygon

The geocoded point is tested against EPA Community Water System service-area boundaries to find the PWSID most likely serving that location.

3

Step 3 — PWSID lookup

Once we have a PWSID, we retrieve the public system profile: name, type, population served, service connections, and boundary source.

4

Step 4 — SDWIS compliance check

We check SDWIS/ECHO for the most recent compliance and monitoring status of the matched system, and link to the Consumer Confidence Report when available.

5

Step 5 — Deterministic recommendation

Your quiz answers and the public data snapshot are passed through a rules-based engine. The engine is deterministic: the same inputs always return the same recommendation.

Where real EPA integration connects

lib/geo.ts — geocodeAddress()

Replace the Nashville stub with a call to a geocoding service (e.g., EPA GeoPlatform, US Census geocoder, or a commercial provider).

lib/geo.ts — matchWaterSystem()

Replace with a point-in-polygon query against the EPA Community Water System ArcGIS feature service.

lib/epa.ts — fetchComplianceData()

Replace with queries to EPA ECHO REST API or SDWIS bulk data to fetch real compliance events for a PWSID.

ClearFlow uses public EPA and local utility data. A home test is recommended before making household-specific treatment decisions.
